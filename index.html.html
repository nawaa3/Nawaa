About NAWA'A

NAWA'A is a youth-led, non-profit organization dedicated to empowering young people aged 13–18 through education, financial literacy, economic awareness, and business education. We help young people understand the world of finance and business while making opportunities more accessible to those who need them.

Through workshops, events, collaborative projects, and educational initiatives, we strive to create meaningful opportunities for young people to learn, develop practical skills, and make a positive impact in their communities.
